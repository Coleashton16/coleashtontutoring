Mentorship is intended for conceptual support and clinical preparation. You are strictly forbidden from sharing active exams, requesting live quiz assistance, or utilizing materials to bypass educational standards.

Intellectual Property & Copyrights

All study guides, practice calculations, path charts, and feedback notes distributed by Cole Ashton are proprietary intellectual property. Distribution, resale, or unauthorized sharing of these files is prohibited.

Attendance & No-Show Regulations

Missed sessions prevent other students from scheduling guidance. Standard attendance dictates cancellations must be submitted 24 hours prior. Unexcused consecutive no-shows can result in direct account restriction.
