Academic Guidelines & Agreement

Please review and accept to continue to your student portal

1. Academic Integrity & Anti-Cheating

These tutoring resources are designed strictly for educational review and test-preparation purposes. Students are strictly prohibited from using these sessions, tutoring materials, or shared answers to cheat on college assignments, tests, or clinical evaluations.

2. Proprietary & Copyrighted Materials

All study guides, custom test questions, nursing flashcards, lectures, and resources provided during mentorship are copyrighted proprietary assets of Cole Ashton. Sharing, publishing online, or distributing these materials outside this portal without prior written authorization is strictly prohibited.

3. Fair Use & Attendance Policy

In order to offer all nursing students equal access, booked times must be respected. "No-shows" or cancellations made less than 24 hours prior to the session prevent other students from booking. Accumulating three consecutive no-shows or late cancellations may result in administrative account suspension.

By clicking agree, you confirm adherence to these policies.

Core ADN Tutoring Subjects

High-yield clinical topics designed around the Associate Degree in Nursing curriculum.
